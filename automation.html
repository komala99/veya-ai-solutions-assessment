How each stage works

Simple automation, human follow-up.

01

Capture

The landing page sends the completed form to an n8n webhook.

02

Validate

n8n checks required information before any CRM record is created.

03

Store

Airtable acts as the lightweight lead record and status source.

04

Tag

Campaign and consultation tags make source and intent visible to the team.

05

Alert

The clinic team gets a real-time notification so no enquiry is left unseen.

06

Follow Up

A confirmation message gives the lead a clear next step without pressure.

07

Book

Once a consultation is booked, the CRM status is updated automatically.

08

Recover

Validation or delivery failures are logged and surfaced for manual action.
